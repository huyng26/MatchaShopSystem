Preparation Guide

Standardized steps for atelier quality control

01

Matcha Sifting

Sift 5g of ceremonial matcha into a warmed chawan. Add 20ml of 80°C water and whisk vigorously in a 'W' motion until a dense, fine froth forms with no large bubbles.

02

Base Combination

Combine chilled oat milk and brown sugar syrup in the serving glass. Add filtered ice up to 70% of the volume. Gently pour the whisked matcha over the back of a spoon to create a layer.

03

Foam Crowning

Slowly top with 40ml of chilled cream cheese foam. The foam should sit cleanly on the matcha layer without immediate bleeding. Dust lightly with matcha powder for garnish.
